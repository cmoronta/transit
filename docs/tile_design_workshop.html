Tile & Screen Workshop: Bike Navigator

Revision 2 · Material 3 for Wear OS

Material 3 for Wear OS: tonal color roles, full-pill shapes, an edge button that hugs the bottom of the round screen, and a time label at the top. The navigation green is a tertiary color role, a nod to Google Maps.

Decisions so far

  • Idle tile: Home · Search · Work. Gym and "More places" removed. A chevron leads to Recent trips.
  • Navigating: Maps-classic turn card. The Ride screen adds a Map view you can drag, switched with an edge button.
  • No glance test. The Ride screen stays visible on the Always-On Display, kept alive by an Ongoing Activity.
  • More generous rerouting: automatic after 15 s off route, at most 1 reroute per 20 s, up to 10 per trip.
  • Arrived and Phone disconnected are approved as they are.

Platform constraints shaping these designs

  • Tiles can't scroll and can't host a live map. The tile chevron opens the app's start screen, scrolled to Recents. The draggable map lives on the Ride screen.
  • Tiles don't appear on the Always-On Display; the watch face does. Always-on navigation is the Ride screen in ambient mode.
  • Map: the Google Maps SDK for Android supports Wear OS and ambient mode. Mobile SDK map loads cost nothing, but it needs a separate Maps API key.
  • Ambient rules: mostly black pixels, no filled color blocks, no buttons. Content shifts slightly to protect the screen from burn-in, and updates come about once per GPS fix.
